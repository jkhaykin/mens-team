Tools

Resources we use in group to help men notice, name, and share what’s going on inside.

Emotions Wheel

Most of us default to a handful of words: fine, good, stressed, mad. The wheel starts with core emotions at the center (happy, sad, angry, fearful, surprised, disgusted, bad) and moves outward to more specific feelings. When you check in or share in group, start at the center and work outward until you land on the word that fits.
